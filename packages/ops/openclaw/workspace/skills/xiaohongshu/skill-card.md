## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The local compliance checker is rules-based and is not an official platform ruling or legal review. <br>
Mitigation: Use the checker as a drafting aid, then review content against Xiaohongshu's official tools, platform policies, and any applicable legal or brand requirements before publishing. <br>
Risk: Generated social content may still contain unsupported promotional claims, medical or efficacy assertions, or off-platform diversion cues. <br>
Mitigation: Run the included pre-publish checker, revise flagged language into specific and supportable wording, and keep a human review step before release. <br>
Risk: Requests for scraping, fake engagement, bulk account activity, or off-platform diversion can create platform and abuse risk. <br>
Mitigation: Keep use to drafting, review, diagnostics, and planning workflows; do not use the skill to develop scraping, fake engagement, or account manipulation tactics. <br>
